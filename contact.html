Wall Prep 101

Ensuring your surface is ready for a masterpiece. Follow these simple steps before we arrive.

1

Clean Surface

The wall should be free of dust, grease, and cobwebs. A simple wipe-down with a damp cloth a day before is usually enough.

2

Minor Repairs

Patch any significant holes or cracks. While we can paint over minor textures, a smooth surface yields the sharpest results.

3

Clear the Way

Please move furniture at least 5-6 feet away from the wall. We'll handle the drop-cloths and surface protection once we arrive.

Not sure about your wall?

Send us a photo of the surface and we'll give you specific advice.
