Travel & Logistics

Projects within a 50-mile radius of the studio include free travel. For further locations, a standard travel fee applies.

Interstate and international projects require accommodation and flight coverage.

Commercial Licensing

Residential murals are for personal enjoyment. Commercial murals include a standard limited-usage license for marketing.

Full buy-outs or merchandise licensing can be negotiated separately.

Equipment & Permits

For walls over 12ft, specialized equipment (lifts or scaffolding) may be required. We handle all rental and permit logistics.

Rental costs are billed at cost plus a 10% management fee.
