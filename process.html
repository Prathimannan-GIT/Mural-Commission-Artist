Our Process

Step-by-step from initial vision to a finished masterpiece. We ensure a seamless and collaborative experience.

1. Submit Your Idea

Fill out our booking form with your wall size, location, and a brief description of your vision. Attach any reference images that inspire you.

2. Free Consultation

We'll schedule a call or site visit to discuss the project in detail, evaluate the wall condition, and provide a formal quote.

3. Design Mockup

Based on our discussion, I create digital sketches superimposed on photos of your wall, so you can see exactly how the final piece will look.

4. On-Site Painting

The creative work begins! I bring all materials and tools, ensuring a clean and professional workspace throughout the painting phase.

5. Final Reveal & Care

A final walk-through, varnishing for protection, and a care guide to ensure your mural stays vibrant for decades.
