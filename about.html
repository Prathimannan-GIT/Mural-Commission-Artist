Meet the Artist

Elias Vaughn

With over a decade of experience in the urban art scene, Elias Vaughn has transformed hundreds of blank walls into vibrant landmarks. His work blends classical techniques with modern street art aesthetics.

Get in Touch View Portfolio

Experience & Philosophy

Artistic Philosophy

I believe every wall has a story to tell. My goal is to listen to the space and the owner to create something that feels like it has always belonged there.

My work is heavily influenced by the interplay between architectural geometry and organic forms. I use light and shadow not just as technical tools, but as emotional anchors that shift with the day's passing.

Education

BFA in Fine Arts from the School of Visual Arts, New York. Specialization in Large-Scale Painting and Urban Design.

Inside the Studio

Located in the heart of the creative district, our studio is more than just a workspace—it's a laboratory for texture and light.

Here, we test pigments, experiment with new protective sealants, and draft life-sized concepts before they ever touch your wall. We invite prospective clients to visit and see the materials and scale of our work firsthand.

  • High-End Spray Lab
  • Custom Pigment Mixing
