Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

At Mural Art Commission, we take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our website. We are committed to ensuring that your personal data is handled in a way that respects your rights and the law.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Personal identifiers (Name, Email, Phone Number)
  • Project details and artistic preferences
  • Financial information for transaction processing (managed by third-party processors)
  • Communication history between you and the artist

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely for:

  • Fulfilling your commission requests and providing artistic services
  • Improving our website experience and customer support
  • Sending project updates and (if opted-in) marketing newsletters
  • Complying with legal and safety requirements

4. Data Security

We implement enterprise-grade security measures including SSL encryption and restricted data access to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us at hello@muralartshop.com.
